# Visual assets

- `plume-logo.jpg` and `plume-mark.png`: supplied project identity, preserved.
- `moon.jpg`: NASA/Goddard/Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (Ernie Wright; LRO/LROC/LOLA data). Downloaded 2026-10-25 from https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg (2048×1024). Source and credits: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/. Used for the orbital visualization only; NASA describes this rendering map as optimized for aesthetics rather than scientific applications. It does not replace the backend's synthetic terrain. No NASA endorsement is implied.
- Surface regolith material, lander and infrastructure are code-generated illustrative geometry/materials. They are not engineering CAD. Asset symbols are enlarged for readability.
- Terrain elevations and hazard overlays come from the PLUME backend. Synthetic scenario data is labeled in the application.
- Fonts are installed locally through Fontsource; Inter, Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono retain their package license files.
